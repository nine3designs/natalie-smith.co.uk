04 04 / 09

E Squared Coffee Corner

A bold, welcoming identity for a local coffee shop with personality.

A full branding project covering identity design through to signage and product mockups. At the centre sits a bold, condensed serif wordmark, E SQUARED, with a coffee-bean detail worked into the O, a hand-drawn cup icon and an “EST. 2026” subline for a grounded, established feel despite being a new opening. A circular “COFFEE CORNER” ring wraps the mark, built to work on round signage as well as cup stickers and packaging.

Advanced Assessment & Solutions

A brand refresh and full website rebuild, from Canva hold-together to a presence Sara is proud of.

Sara came to Nine3Designs after a run of disappointing experiences with previous designers. Her website was a basic Canva build that no longer reflected the professionalism of her role, and she had no way to access or manage her own domain. We began with the foundations: her logo rebuilt as a clean, scalable version, a considered colour palette in place of the muted greys, and social graphics for a consistent look. Then the biggest step, a complete website rebuild, fully under her control and consistent with her wider branding.

Lopwiel

An outdoor brand built to move — from app icon to adventure kit.

Lopwiel is an outdoor brand built around the descent — start the descent and keep going. Logo Design · Legal
09 09 / 09

Tevuna Law

A confident, modern mark for real attorneys and flat-fee clarity.

Tevuna Law is a legal brand built on straight answers and clear pricing. The mark draws on classical architecture — a stylised column portico — giving the identity instant authority and a sense of establishment, while clean geometric line work keeps it modern rather than traditional. Deep navy sets a serious, trustworthy tone, lifted by a strong blue that feels sharp and approachable. Paired with bold, spaced capitals and the positioning line 'Real attorneys | Strategic answers | Flat fees', the result is a logo that feels solid, direct and built on trust.

DeliverablesLogo suite · Flyer · Reception signage · Brand guidelines
