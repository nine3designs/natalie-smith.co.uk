The designer

World class,
by design.

I'm Natalie, founder of Nine3 Designs, based in Sheffield, South Yorkshire. Before design, my world was serious competitive martial arts: representing Team GB and becoming World Champion twice.

Then a major change in my health, my heart stopping, a pacemaker, and later an ADHD diagnosis, took my life in a completely different direction. It led me here, to design: the same discipline, focus and drive, pointed at building brands people remember.

Every project gets the treatment I'd want as a client: clear communication, honest advice, and artwork that's production-ready, not just beautiful on screen.
